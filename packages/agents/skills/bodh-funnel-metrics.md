# BODH funnel metrics

The funnel: **click → registration → activation → paid**.
- Registration: account created in the BODH app.
- Activation: the user generated their first kundli (birth chart). This is the north-star metric for marketing, because activated users are the ones who later pay.
- Paid: first purchase (report, subscription or consultation).

Attribution is first touch: each user is credited to the channel and campaign of their first tracked visit (UTMs, ad click ids). Organic social without UTMs shows up as Direct or Organic Social, so organic numbers are undercounted.

**Judge channels by cost per activated user (CPA, in ₹)**, then activation rate (activations / registrations) and paid rate. Clicks and registrations alone are vanity. Compare with the previous period of the same length, not with one good day. A channel needs at least about 30 activations in the period before you call it better or worse than another; below that, say the data is thin.

**Good signs**: CPA falling while volume holds; activation rate above about 40%. **Warning signs**: registrations up but activation rate down (low-intent traffic or a broken onboarding step); spend concentrated in one ad set.

Spend changes, bids and budgets are not yours to make. You recommend; people approve in the app, and the guardrails enforce caps.

When the numbers are missing (no tracking data yet), say so plainly and base the plan on content and search data instead. Never invent numbers.
