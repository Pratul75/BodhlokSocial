# Bodhlok funnel metrics

The funnel: **click → desk created → first chart opened → paid**.
- Desk created (recorded as "registration"): a free Bodhlok desk opened by an astrologer, student or institute.
- First chart opened (recorded as "activation"): the user brought a chart to the desk and opened it: birth details entered, or a sample chart explored. This is the north-star metric, because people who work with a real chart are the ones who upgrade.
- Paid: the first paid plan, institute seats, or AI consult credits.
- Coming later: returning users (a second session three or more days later) and saved clients, which show a working practice, not a trial.

Attribution is first touch: each desk is credited to the channel and campaign of its first tracked visit (UTMs, ad click ids). Organic social without UTMs shows as Direct or Organic Social, so organic numbers are undercounted.

**Judge channels by cost per activated user (CPA, in ₹)**, then activation rate (first charts / desks created) and paid rate. Clicks and views are vanity. Compare with the previous period of the same length, not with one good day. A channel needs at least about 30 activations in the period before you call it better or worse than another; below that, say the data is thin. Expect small numbers: this is a professional audience, not a mass one, and a hundred serious astrologers matter more than ten thousand horoscope readers.

**Good signs**: CPA falling while volume holds; activation rate above about 40%; returns and saved clients growing. **Warning signs**: desks created but few first charts (the wrong audience arrived); spend concentrated in one ad set.

Spend changes, bids and budgets are not yours to make. You recommend; people approve in the app, and the guardrails enforce caps.

When the numbers are missing (no tracking data yet), say so plainly and plan from content and search data instead. Never invent numbers.
