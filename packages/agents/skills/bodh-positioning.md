# Bodhlok positioning (from the launch film)

**Tagline:** Professional Astrology. Reimagined. **Closing line:** Ancient Wisdom. Structured Intelligence. Human Judgement. **Brand line:** Ancient knowledge deserves modern tools.

## Who it is for, and who it is not for
For practising astrologers, serious astrology students, teachers, institutes, and anyone who wants to study Jyotish systematically and deeply. "Deliberately professional users." Not for someone who wants a two-minute random prediction. Bodhlok is not an entertainment astrology app. Alok Sir's brief: "Don't create something people can just play with. Create something serious."

## The problem it solves
A professional astrologer reading a chart holds many layers at once: lagna, navamsha, dasha, graha strength, gochar, and which classical rule actually applies to this person's question. The problem is not a shortage of information. It is seeing the right information, at the right time, in the right context. Bodhlok reorganises those screens into one clean desk.

## Three words
**Calculate. Connect. Communicate.** Accurate calculations; meaningful connections between them; communicated to the astrologer in understandable language.

## How the AI works (say it exactly like this)
Four layers, in order:
1. **Calculation engine**: planetary positions, houses, divisional charts, dashas, strengths. The LLM does not calculate anything.
2. **Astrology rule engine**: classical principles and Asttrolok's teaching framework decide which rules apply to the available data.
3. **Context engine**: the question decides what matters. A career question pulls D1, career houses and lords, D10, strengths, running mahadasha and antardasha, relevant transits. A relationship question pulls a different set.
4. **Language model**: turns that structured, already-evaluated information into readable language, in Hindi or English.

"The LLM does not invent astrology. Bodhlok gives the LLM astrology." Calculation first, rules second, context third, language last.

**The core claim:** AI with the astrologer. Not AI instead of the astrologer. The diagnostic-machine analogy: it gives the doctor better information; the decision stays with the doctor. Better information. Better context. Human judgement. The final judgement is always the astrologer's, because the person in front of them is a human being with circumstances, not a kundli.

## The demo sequence (the visual signature)
Question → Relevant charts → Dasha → Strength → Rules → Context → Language output. Every product demo, reel and walkthrough follows this order on a sample chart. Never a static screen for more than 5 to 7 seconds; zoom into what is being discussed.

## Knowledge foundation
The astrological foundation follows Asttrolok founder Alok Khandelwal's guidance and teaching methodology; Asttrolok students and practising astrologers tested it on real charts and consultation situations. Jyotish's rules are not new and must not be new: Parashara and Jaimini stay as they are. What is new is how they are seen, connected and used.

## What the desk holds (name real features, show real screens)
D1 with Bhava Chalit, Sudarshan Chakra and the Shodashavarga; KP with 249 sub-lords, ruling planets and horary by number; Jaimini chara karakas, arudha padas and Chara dasha; Vimshottari, Ashtottari and Yogini to the pratyantar; yogas, doshas, Shadbala, Ashtakavarga, Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, Panch Pakshi; Muhurta, marriage muhurta (Tarabala, Chandrabala, Gurubala, Graha Maitri), Varshaphala, Prashna, kundli matching and remedies; client notes and consultation history; AI consult in Hindi or English; runs in the browser, continuously updated, desktop-first (mobile is for quick reference; serious consultation happens at a larger screen).

## Tone and visuals
Premium, intelligent, calm, confident. Conversational, as if speaking to intelligent astrologers in an auditorium. Avoid mystical, flashy or "AI futuristic". The actual Bodhlok UI is the hero: whenever a claim is made about what the product does, show the product doing it. Never generic galaxies, rotating zodiac wheels, glowing sages, excessive temples, artificial AI brains or neon astrology graphics. Open with the problem, reveal the logo after. Let the closing thought land.

## Lines that must never appear
Predictions for the reader ("your future", "your love life", "what the stars say for you"); "AI astrologer", "no astrologer needed", AI replacing astrologers; guarantees or 100% accuracy; fear, curses, doshas as doom; remedies for sale. The planning and muhurta modules are astrological guidance only, never medical diagnosis, fertility prediction, contraception or healthcare advice: say "Astrological guidance only. Not medical advice." whenever health, pregnancy or fertility timing comes up.

## Privacy is a professional responsibility
Birth details and consultation notes are private. Content never shows a real client's chart; use sample charts and say so.
