# Bodhlok brand voice

**What Bodhlok is.** Professional Vedic astrology software in the browser, built by Retrograde Technologies ("Professional Astrology Reimagined"). One desk brings together chart calculations (Swiss Ephemeris, ayanamsa of the user's choice), D1 with Bhava Chalit and the Shodashavarga, KP with sub-lords and horary, Jaimini, Vimshottari, Ashtottari and Yogini dashas, Ashtakavarga, Shadbala, Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, Prashna, Muhurta, Varshaphala, kundli matching, client notes and consultation history. An AI consult box answers questions about a chart in English or Hindi and uses credits. People start with a free desk and move to paid plans.

**Who it is for.** Learners of Jyotish, practising astrologers, and institutes that teach. Not the general public looking for predictions. Speak to someone who knows what a dasha is, or wants to.

**Sound like** a senior Jyotishi who also likes good tools: calm, precise, generous with knowledge, respectful of the tradition and of the astrologer's judgement. Bodhlok's own line: "Ancient wisdom. Structured intelligence. Human judgement."

**Do**
- Show real chart work: a placement in D1 and D9, a KP sub-lord, a dasha timeline, a Prashna chart. Teach one technique per post and show where it lives in the desk.
- Use Sanskrit terms correctly, with a short gloss for learners: "Bhava Chalit (house cusps)", "pratyantar (third-level dasha)".
- Write in English by default. Hindi posts are welcome; Hinglish only for Reel and Short hooks. Never mix scripts within a word.
- Frame AI as an assistant to the astrologer: "Consult AI with the astrologer, not instead of the astrologer."
- One call to action per post: "Open your free desk at bodhlok.com", "Start free", or "Try this chart in Bodhlok".

**Don't**
- Make predictions for the reader or promise outcomes (marriage, money, health). No fear, no curses, no doshas as doom.
- Sell remedies, gemstones or pujas.
- Claim the AI replaces astrologers, or that any result is certain.
- Use clickbait ("you won't believe"), ALL CAPS words, or more than two emoji.
- Name or criticise competing software.

**Hashtags**: three to six, mixing the brand kit defaults (#Bodhlok #VedicAstrology #Jyotish) with one or two topical tags (#KPAstrology, #Dasha, #Nakshatra). LinkedIn: at most three.
