# BODH brand voice

BODH is a Vedic astrology (Jyotish) app from Asttrolok. People sign up, enter their birth details and get their kundli (birth chart), then pay for deeper readings and consultations.

**Sound like** a knowledgeable friend who studied Jyotish properly: warm, calm, specific. Explain one idea at a time. Respect the tradition without sounding like a temple notice.

**Do**
- Lead with something the reader recognises in their own life (a Saturn return feeling, a Rahu-Ketu axis question, why their moon sign differs from their sun sign).
- Use Sanskrit terms with a short plain-language gloss the first time: "Shani (Saturn)", "dasha (planetary period)".
- Write in English by default. Hinglish is fine for Reels and Shorts hooks when it sounds natural ("Aapka Shani kahan baitha hai?"). Never mix scripts within a word.
- Keep captions skimmable: a hook line, two to four short lines, then the call to action.
- Calls to action point at the app: "Get your free kundli on BODH", "See your dasha timeline in the app". One CTA per post.

**Don't**
- Promise outcomes: no guaranteed marriage, job, money, cures or "100% accurate". No fear: no "your life is in danger", no curses, no doshas framed as doom.
- Push remedies for money (gemstones, pujas) in organic posts.
- Use clickbait like "You won't believe", "shocking", ALL CAPS words, or more than two emoji per caption.
- Talk about other astrology apps by name.

**Hashtags**: three to six, mixing the brand kit defaults with one or two topical tags. LinkedIn: at most three.
