# Bodhlok brand voice

**What Bodhlok is.** Professional Vedic astrology software in the browser, built by Retrograde Technologies on Asttrolok's teaching foundation. "Professional Astrology. Reimagined." See bodh-positioning for who it is for, the AI story and the demo sequence.

**Sound like** a senior Jyotishi who also builds good tools: calm, precise, generous with knowledge, respectful of the tradition and of the astrologer's judgement. Speak to someone who knows what a dasha is, or is studying to. Never to the public looking for a prediction.

**Do**
- Open with the astrologer's real problem (time lost in calculation, information scattered across screens, rules that don't apply to the question), then show the desk solving it.
- Show real chart work on a sample chart: a placement in D1 and D9, a KP sub-lord, a dasha timeline, Ashtakavarga bindus, a Prashna chart. One technique per post, and where it lives in the desk.
- Use Sanskrit terms correctly with a short gloss for students: "Bhava Chalit (house cusps)", "pratyantar (third-level dasha)".
- English by default; Hindi posts are welcome; Hinglish where it sounds like the film ("Aapka Shani kahan baitha hai?" is fine as a hook for astrologers, not as a horoscope).
- Frame AI exactly as the film does: calculation, rules, context, then language; "AI with the astrologer, not instead of the astrologer."
- One call to action per post: "Open your free desk at bodhlok.com", "Start free", or "Try this on a sample chart in Bodhlok".

**Don't**
- Make predictions for the reader or promise outcomes. No fear, no curses, no doshas as doom.
- Sell remedies, gemstones or pujas.
- Claim the AI replaces astrologers, calculates, or is certain of anything.
- Use clickbait ("you won't believe"), ALL CAPS words, more than two emoji, or mystical and "cosmic" language.
- Name or criticise competing software in posts (comparison pages on the website are the place for that, factual and fair).

**Hashtags**: three to six, from the brand kit defaults (#Bodhlok #VedicAstrology #Jyotish) plus one or two topical ones (#KPAstrology, #Dasha, #Ashtakavarga). LinkedIn: at most three.
